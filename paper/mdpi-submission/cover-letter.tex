Dear Editors,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, ``HARK: Human-Accountable, Authority-Typed Review Knowledge for Evidence-Bearing Document Review,'' for consideration in \emph{Computers}.

The manuscript presents an executable architecture for rule-governed document review that combines rule-specific operational authority, source-located evidence, explicit pending outcomes, and recorded human disposition. On a fixed corpus of 2,000 technical proposals containing 5,323 planted defects, the combined configuration achieves micro-F1 0.746, compared with 0.205 for the keyword baseline, and 75.4\% agreement with policy-derived verdicts. The study also analyses evidence-gate acceptance and unresolved review workload, and specifies deployment contracts for authenticated approval, comprehensive authority control, and configuration recovery.

We believe the work will interest readers in human-centred artificial intelligence, neuro-symbolic systems, document intelligence, and dependable review workflows. The manuscript distinguishes the evaluated prototype from deployment-level requirements and positions HARK as a regression-tested review aid rather than an autonomous decision system. The rule package, evaluation scripts, and released evaluation materials are available through the repository and archived release reported in the manuscript.
